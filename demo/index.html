BTC Iron Condor Backtest

An iron condor is an options trading strategy. It sells a put below the price and a call above it, and buys one further out on each side as protection. It collects a credit up front and keeps it if BTC stays between the two sold strikes.

This backtest opens a 50-day condor at the 12-delta strikes with $1,000 wings. It reprices the position every hour with Black-Scholes. I had no historical implied volatility data for the options, so implied volatility is calculated from the DVOL index. It takes a 4% haircut on entry and exit. A trade closes at the profit target, when a sold strike's delta passes the exit level, or at 21 days to expiry. After a loss it can sit out a cooldown.
